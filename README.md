## 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm a **Computer Science & Engineering student, software developer, and product builder from India**. I enjoy taking ideas beyond prototypes and turning them into complete, deployable systems — from **architecture and backend APIs to frontend experiences, cloud infrastructure, deployment, operations, analytics, and product growth**.

My work currently sits at the intersection of **software engineering, cloud/serverless architecture, commerce, healthcare technology, automation, and business systems**.

- 🎓 Pursuing **B.Tech in Computer Science & Engineering** at **DIET under MAKAUT**.
- 💻 Working as a **Software Developer Intern at HealthHepta**, contributing to healthcare software and role-based applications.
- 🚀 Building and evolving **OgenSync**, **QuiqVeg**, and **Medik**.
- 🧩 Interested in building **complete products**, not only isolated frontend or backend components.
- ☁️ Designing **Cloudflare-first architectures** using Workers, D1, R2, KV, Durable Objects, Queues, caching, and edge APIs.
- 📱 Building for **Web, PWA, React Native mobile apps, and Electron desktop apps**.
- ⚡ Exploring **realtime systems, background sync, distributed architecture, event-driven systems, caching, WebSockets, and offline-first experiences**.
- 🔐 Working with **JWT authentication, RBAC, authorization, secure APIs, OTP flows, session management, and multi-role platforms**.
- 📊 Interested in the product side too: **analytics, automation, operations, monetization, retention, growth, and business strategy**.
- 🧠 I learn by **building, testing, breaking, fixing, deploying, and improving real systems**.

> **Current direction:** Build fast, reliable, scalable software that solves practical business problems and works well even on low-end devices and unreliable networks.
