# 🚀 Flagship Product — QuiqVeg

**QuiqVeg** is a hyperlocal commerce and delivery platform I have been building and continuously rebuilding as the product and architecture evolved.

The project has progressed through multiple generations:

```text
Early commerce experiments
        ↓
My Commerce
        ↓
QuiqVeg
        ↓
QuiqVeg Rebuild
        ↓
Multi-region production platform
```

The current system goes beyond a basic ordering application and includes engineering around:

- 🛒 Restaurant, grocery, vegetable, and everyday-needs commerce
- 📍 Location-aware services and regional availability
- 🚚 Store, customer, delivery-partner, and operational workflows
- 👥 Multi-role applications and authorization
- 📱 PWA and React Native customer experiences
- ⚡ Realtime operations and notifications
- 🗺️ Routing and delivery-distance workflows
- ☁️ Regional and Cloudflare-based backend architecture
- 🔄 Background processing, caching, and synchronization
- 📊 Operational and business workflows

QuiqVeg has been one of my biggest engineering learning environments — forcing me to work on **product decisions, architecture, backend systems, deployment, mobile experiences, operations, performance, and real-world edge cases together**.
